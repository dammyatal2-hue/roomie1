Data Deletion Instructions

If you would like your personal data deleted from Roomie (meetyourroomie.com), you can request deletion as follows.

Email Request

To request deletion of your account and data, whether you signed up with email or with Facebook Login:

  1. Send an email to dammyatal2@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request."
  2. Include the email address or Facebook account associated with your Roomie account.
  3. We will confirm your request and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Revoking Facebook Access Directly

You can also revoke Roomie's access to your Facebook account at any time through Facebook's own settings:

  1. Go to Facebook Settings & Privacy → Settings.
  2. Select Apps and Websites.
  3. Find Roomie in your list of connected apps.
  4. Click Remove.

Removing the app through Facebook will stop Roomie from receiving any further data from your Facebook account. To fully delete data already stored in our systems, please also submit a request using the email method above.

What Gets Deleted

Upon a verified deletion request, we will delete:

  • Your account and profile information
  • Your listings and booking requests
  • Your messages
  • Any community content tied to your account (or anonymize it where deletion would affect other users' conversations)

Some information may be retained for a limited period where required by law or for legitimate business purposes (e.g., fraud prevention records), but will not be used for any other purpose.

Contact

For questions about data deletion, contact us at dammyatal2@gmail.com.
